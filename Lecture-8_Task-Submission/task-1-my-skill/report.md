# Task 1 Report

## Project title and what it does
Professional Email Writer Skill — this skill turns rough notes into a polished, professional email draft with a subject line, greeting, body, and closing.

## AI tools and apps used
- Claude.ai

## Prompts used and how they were refined
1. Initial prompt: “Create a skill that writes professional emails from rough notes.”
2. Refined prompt: “Make the email concise, formal, and ready to send.”
3. Final prompt: “Use a polite tone, include a subject line, and keep the email brief.”

## How I tested or verified it worked
I tested the skill by giving it natural prompts such as “Write a professional email about this” and checked whether it produced a consistent professional format.

## What worked, what did not, and problems faced
- Worked well: the skill produced a clear structure and professional tone.
- Did not work perfectly at first: the early version needed clearer instructions about being brief and formal.
- Problem faced: the skill needed refinement to avoid overly long drafts.

## Why I chose this skill and how it helps daily life
I chose this skill because I often need to write professional messages quickly for class, work, or formal follow-ups. It saves time and keeps my tone consistent.
